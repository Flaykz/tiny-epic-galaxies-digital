# BGG announcement (draft)

**Suggested forum:** Tiny Epic Galaxies → Variants / Fan Creations (or General).
**Suggested title:** Free fan-made digital Tiny Epic Galaxies (browser, solo + AI + online) — bring your own VASSAL art

---

Hi all,

I built a free, fan-made **digital version of Tiny Epic Galaxies** that runs in your browser — no install, nothing to buy from me. It's a love letter to the game, and a way to get a quick game in when the box isn't handy.

**Play it:** https://tiny-epic-galaxies.pages.dev
**Source (open, MIT):** https://github.com/johnchampaign/tiny-epic-galaxies-digital

**What's in it**
- **Solo vs. the Rogue Galaxy**, **local hotseat**, **vs. AI**, and **online async multiplayer** (create a game, share a link per player — play at your own pace).
- The full turn engine: roll dice by empire level, the six die actions (move / acquire energy / acquire culture / advance diplomacy / advance economy / utilize colony), orbiting & colonizing, empire upgrades, the Converter, rerolls, **following**, secret missions, and the 21-VP endgame.
- Quality-of-life: take-back/undo for local games, confirm before abandoning an orbit, hover-to-enlarge planet cards, and a one-click "report a problem / send game log" so I can fix issues fast.

**About the art — important**
This project **does not include or distribute any of Gamelyn's artwork.** Instead, you bring your own copy: the app asks you to download the official **VASSAL module** and pick the `.vmod` file; it extracts the images **in your browser** and caches them locally. Nothing is uploaded, and the public site ships **zero** game art (there's also a clean text/no-art mode if you'd rather not bother). The card/mission *data* is my own transcription for the rules logic.

**Please support the real thing.** This is a fan project and no substitute for the physical game — Tiny Epic Galaxies is fantastic in the box. If you don't own it, buy it from Gamelyn Games. If the publisher would prefer I change or take down anything, just say the word and I will.

It's early and I'm actively fixing things — feedback, bug reports, and rules nitpicks are very welcome (there's a "Report a problem" button right in the app). Hope you enjoy it!

— John
